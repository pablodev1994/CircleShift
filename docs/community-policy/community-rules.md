# Community rules

CircleShift is a place to trade short, practical help using time credits. These rules apply to every member, coordinator, moderator and admin. Moderators enforce them; the product is built so the rules can be checked in code where possible.

## Who can join

- **Adults only (18+).** The MVP has no accounts for minors. A member confirms they are an adult when they create a profile; the API rejects a profile without `adult_confirmed = true`.
- The demo uses **fictional profiles only**. Seed data never contains real names, contact details or photos of real people.
- No identity checks or background checks are performed, and CircleShift does not promise that any member is trustworthy. Members decide for themselves whom to meet.

## What you can offer and request

- Short, practical skills: language practice, bike repair, résumé reviews, gardening tips, coding help, cooking basics and similar.
- One exchange covers one session of 15 to 120 minutes.
- **Not allowed:** paid services or anything involving money, regulated professional advice presented as such (medical, legal, financial), dating or companionship requests, anything illegal, and anything involving minors.

## How to behave

1. Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech, threats or sexual content.
2. Keep contact on the platform until both people have accepted an exchange. Do not ask for phone numbers, home addresses or social media handles in public posts.
3. Show up, or cancel early. Repeated no-shows can be reported.
4. Confirm completion honestly. Only confirm a session that really happened.
5. No spam: no repeated posts, mass invitations or links unrelated to the skill.

## Location and privacy

- CircleShift stores at most a coarse, optional area label (for example "Medellín – Laureles") and a remote/in-person preference. It never stores GPS coordinates or location history.
- Profiles are private until the member chooses to make them visible.
- Blocking someone hides you from each other everywhere: search, match suggestions, invitations and messages.

## Time credits

- One minute of help earns one minute of credit; the person who received help spends the same amount.
- Credits have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold or transferred outside a completed exchange.
- New members receive a small, clearly labelled bootstrap grant so they can request help before offering it.

## Reporting and moderation

- Any member can report a post, profile or message and pick a reason.
- Automated checks may **flag** content for review, with a reason and a confidence score. A flag is never a ban: a human moderator always makes the decision and records a reason code.
- Possible outcomes: no action, warning, content hidden, or account suspended. The author is told the outcome and the reason.
- Every decision can be appealed once. A different moderator reviews the appeal and can see the full history.

## Not an emergency service

CircleShift is not for emergencies or urgent safety situations. If someone is in danger, contact local emergency services.
